# Design source

`claude-design/` is the Claude Design canvas "Crystal Royal Apartment — Booking Site Design"
(https://claude.ai/artifact/DRxojYhXcvWwdSvc11A2es), exported as its 19 artboard files plus `canvas.json`
(artboard titles, sizes and the owner's open-decisions note).

Direction on this canvas: "Faceted Midnight" (midnight #171512 ground, Cormorant Garamond display, gold-filled buttons).
`docs/BOOKING-SITE-BRIEF.md` (v3) withdraws that direction in favour of "Two stones, one gold line"
(Lapis and Jade stones, Jost and DM Sans, no serif). The two disagree; see the open question in the session notes.
